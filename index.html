Message from the Director

Arvind Kumar Singh
Director | Educator | Mathematics Mentor | Academic Leader

Arvind Kumar Singh is an experienced educator, Mathematics mentor and academic leader with 11+ years of teaching and academic experience across Delhi, Patna, Haryana, Rajasthan, Kanpur, Bihar and Jharkhand.

His journey in education is deeply personal. Having completed his schooling in a village and his Class 12 education in Bokaro, he pursued his higher education and degrees from Patna and Delhi.

His journey from a village classroom to teaching and mentoring students across different parts of India has strengthened his belief that:
“Talent is everywhere, but every child deserves the right guidance and opportunity to succeed.”

🌱 THE VISION BEHIND GAURIKULAM INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

As the Director of Gaurikulam International School, Arvind Sir is working towards creating an institution where quality education, modern learning, discipline, values and individual attention come together.

📚 A JOURNEY ACROSS INDIA

During his career, Arvind Sir has worked with several reputed schools, coaching institutions and educational programmes.

He has worked as an Academic Director at Gurukul Academy, Mahendragarh, where he was involved in academic planning, student mentoring and competitive-examination preparation.

He has also worked with various educational institutions across Delhi and North India, including academic centres in: Mukherjee Nagar, Uttam Nagar, Rewari, Narnaul, Chirawa, Jhunjhunu, Sikar, Dadri and Kanpur, where he taught Mathematics to JEE and NDA aspirants.

His professional journey also includes:

  • Bijendra Public School, Purnea
  • C.S.K.M. Public School, Chhattarpur, New Delhi – Project Head for JEE & NEET Preparation
  • A reputed school in Mahendragarh
  • Gurukul Academy, Mahendragarh – Academic Director
  • CCL Vidya Sarathi Project through NCSM – Teaching Mathematics to JEE students at CCL D.A.V. schools
🎓 MENTORING & ACHIEVEMENTS

Over more than a decade in education, Arvind Sir has had the privilege of mentoring thousands of students. He has mentored:

  • 500+ Students who went on to become Engineers
  • 1,500+ Students who qualified for NDA, Navy & Air Force examinations

His teaching approach focuses on conceptual clarity, regular practice, problem-solving skills and building confidence rather than simply memorising formulas or answers.

🏫 PRESENT ACADEMIC RESPONSIBILITIES

Currently, Arvind Kumar Singh serves as an Assistant Professor at Subhash Institute of Technology, where he teaches Mathematics to students of B.Tech | BCA | BBA | Diploma.

He is also the founder and mentor of Gaurikulam Mathematics, where he continues to guide students in Mathematics. Many of his students have consistently achieved 80+ marks in Mathematics, reflecting his focus on strong fundamentals, systematic practice and individual attention.

💡 HIS EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

At the heart of Arvind Sir's teaching philosophy is a simple belief:
“Every Student is a Winner & Every Child is a Good Learner.”

He believes that every child learns differently. Some students may achieve quickly, while others may need more time, encouragement and personal guidance.

For him, the responsibility of a teacher goes beyond completing the syllabus. A teacher should identify potential, remove fear, build confidence and inspire students to believe in themselves.

“Marks can measure performance, but they cannot measure a child's potential.”

Having experienced education in a village and later studied and worked in cities and reputed institutions, he understands the importance of providing students with access to quality guidance irrespective of their background.

His vision is to bring meaningful, affordable and quality education closer to children, particularly those who may not have access to the same opportunities available in larger cities.

At Gaurikulam, his vision is to create an environment where:

  • Every child is valued, understood and encouraged.
  • Every student receives proper guidance and individual attention.
  • Strong academic foundations are developed.
  • Students learn through understanding, curiosity and practical thinking.
  • Discipline and good values grow alongside academic excellence.
  • Parents and teachers work together as partners.
  • Students are prepared not only for examinations, but also for future careers and life.
❤️ A MESSAGE TO STUDENTS

Dear Students,
Never judge your future by your present marks. You may struggle with a subject today, but with the right guidance, consistent practice and determination, you can improve tomorrow.

Dream big. Ask questions. Learn from your mistakes. Respect your parents and teachers. Work hard and never give up.

“Your present result is only one chapter of your story—not the whole story.”

🤝 A MESSAGE TO PARENTS

Dear Parents,
Every child has a different journey, ability and pace of learning. Do not compare your child with another child. Instead, understand their strengths, encourage their efforts and work together with teachers to help them grow.

“A child's success begins when someone believes in them.”

🎯 HIS EDUCATIONAL MISSION

For Arvind Sir, education is not simply about producing high scorers. It is about developing confident learners, responsible citizens, capable professionals and good human beings.

His mission is to create an educational environment where every child can confidently say: “I can learn. I can improve. I can achieve.”

That belief is at the heart of Gaurikulam International School.

“Dream Big. Learn Every Day. Work Hard. Stay Humble. Never Give Up.”
“Every Student is a Winner. Every Child Can Learn. Every Dream Deserves an Opportunity.”

Arvind Kumar Singh
Director, Gaurikulam International School
